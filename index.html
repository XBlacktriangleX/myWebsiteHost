LAND ART

About This Category

Land art merges sculptural practice with the landscape itself — working directly with terrain, vegetation, and natural processes to create works that are inseparable from their environment. This category explores interventions in natural spaces where the site becomes both medium and message, shaped by weather, time, and the elemental forces of the landscape.

SCULPTURE

About This Category

Sculpture is the language of form in space — carving, casting, and constructing three-dimensional works that engage the body and the eye. From traditional stone and metal to experimental materials, each piece emerges from a dialogue between intention and material resistance, revealing forms that exist only through the act of making.

MATERIAL

About This Category

Material research pushes beyond traditional sculptural boundaries — experimenting with living organisms, industrial processes, and unexpected substances to discover new aesthetic and structural possibilities. Each investigation begins with a question: what can this material become, and what does it want to reveal?
